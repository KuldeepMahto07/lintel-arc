ONE PLACE TO DECIDE
WHAT REACHES THE MODEL

Lintel checks every sync against your firm's rules. The dashboard is where those findings become decisions: who fixes what, who signs it off, and what the drawing looked like before.

Every model on one grid

Each model your team syncs shows up as a sheet with its own plan thumbnail, open issue count and changes waiting. Sort by last sync or by trouble, and archive what's finished.

SIGN-OFF QUEUE

Nothing moves unsigned

Fixes from the issue list and edits the assistant drafts land in one queue across all projects. Each shows the element, the value before and after, and the reason, ready to approve or reject.

APPROVE ALL ALWAYS ASKS TO CONFIRM
AUDIT LOG

A record you can show a client

Every sync, decision, applied edit and revert is written down with the person and the time. Any applied change can be reverted, and the revert is logged too.

ROLES

The right person decides

Owners, admins, approvers, members and viewers each see the buttons they can use. Two-approval mode makes a change wait for a second approver before it reaches the model.

Your office standard, as numbers

Keep your firm's rules in one table: a value, what it applies to and the section it comes from. Import them as CSV, switch one off for a project, and activate the rules the assistant suggests.

WORKFLOW

FROM SYNC TO SIGN-OFF,
IN 3 STEPS

The dashboard never edits a drawing on its own. It shows what Lintel found, lets the right person decide, and keeps the record.

STEP [01]

Sync a model

Each designer connects their CAD app with a personal token. Every save sends rooms, doors, stairs and walls to the dashboard, and the checks run straight away.

STEP [02]

Review what's flagged

Each issue names the rule, the measured value, the required value and the section it comes from. One click drafts the fix as a before and after.

STEP [03]

Sign off and record

An approver signs the change. The plugin applies it in one undo step, and the audit log keeps who decided, when, and the values before and after.
